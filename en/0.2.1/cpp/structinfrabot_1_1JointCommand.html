Detailed Description

An RT_JOINT command for J1–J7.

Sent with Robot::servej().

MIT profile: the drives track position and velocity with kp/kd plus the torque feed-forward. Position-velocity profile: the drives move to position at no more than velocity_limit (their v_des; 0 would stop them); velocity, kp, kd and torque are not used.
