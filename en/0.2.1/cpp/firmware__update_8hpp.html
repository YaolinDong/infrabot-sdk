Detailed Description

Signed firmware updates over Ethernet.

These calls use their own maintenance socket on port 5002. They do not need a connected Robot and never enable or disable a drive. They are [Blocking]: run them outside the control loop. Support the arm, disable the drives and disconnect motor power before updating.
