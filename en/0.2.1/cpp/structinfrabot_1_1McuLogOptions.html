Detailed Description

Options of Robot::download_mcu_log().

The board keeps its own run log in SDRAM (firmware with INFRABOT_CAP_RUN_LOG): the text it printed (newest 10 minutes) and its 1 kHz telemetry (newest 10 s). It is lost on power-off or reset.
